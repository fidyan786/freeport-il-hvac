## What ranking pages tend to do

| Pattern | What shows up | What we do instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| City landing pages | A Freeport URL with a service list, a short FAQ, and a town checklist | One real page per topic, plus one service-area page for the villages |
| Thin doorways | A quote form and a town name repeated | No per-town websites |
| Map pack | Pins on Freeport streets, a Lena office, and Rock City street listings | No invented pin. Callers name the town and ZIP |
| Emergency claims | Round-the-clock lines that sit next to weekday office hours | Say the call connects someone. Do not promise a time |
| Prices and brands | Dollar ranges and preferred equipment names | No prices, no equipment brand names, no “best” |

Shorter Freeport city pages often name Cedarville, Dakota, Pearl City, Lena, Orangeville, Rock City, and Ridott, and skip German Valley, Davis, and Winslow. Longer checklists name every village and still describe one Freeport base. Rockford-based pages list Freeport without explaining the village drives.
