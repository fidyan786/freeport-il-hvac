# Image credits

Photographs on this site come from Wikimedia Commons. Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, and Openverse were checked again for residential furnaces, outdoor condensers, civilian technicians, thermostats, ductwork, and Midwest winter homes. Openverse mostly returned the same Commons and Flickr files. A civilian technician photograph was not used because the shirt carried another company’s logo. Military crew photos were not used. The files below are real places and real equipment. They are not photos of a Freeport service call.

Each master was center-cropped to 3:2 and saved as WebP. `public/photos/sized/` holds 480, 640, and 960 pixel WebP and AVIF copies. `public/photos/heroes/` holds a 640-pixel WebP. CC BY-SA adaptations stay under the same license.
