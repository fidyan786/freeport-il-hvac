# Brand system — Millrace Heating & Air

## Name

**Millrace Heating & Air**

Named for the mill races along the Pecatonica in this part of Stephenson County. Sounds like a local mechanical shop, not a directory mill or a family-name incumbent. Short name: **Millrace**. Assistant: **Millrace Assistant**.

Do not file this as a legal clearance. Confirm IL SOS / USPTO before advertising at scale.

## Personality

Reliable · Professional · Local · Capable · Human

Not: SaaS, cheap flyer, corporate robot, “Freeport HVAC Pros.”

## Imagery

Documentary Midwest HVAC: winter houses, basements, outdoor condensers, rooftops. No cheesy smiling stock. No prices on photos. `public/photos/`.

## Voice

Speak to homeowners and small businesses in 61032. Climate math when it helps. Safety before troubleshooting. Never invent years, staff, licenses, 24/7, or ratings.
