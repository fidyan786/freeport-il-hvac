# Freeport HVAC SERP and competitor notes

Researched 13 September 2026 for Millrace Heating & Air. Competitor claims below are **theirs, attributed**. Do not copy copy, branding, reviews, or images.

## Market shape

Freeport (23,973 in 2020; ~23,200 in 2025 estimate) is the Stephenson County seat. NOAA station USC00113262: **6,956 HDD vs 690 CDD**, January ~28/11°F, July ~82/62°F, ~35 in snow. IECC **Zone 5A**. Heating is the money query set. Cooling spikes in short humid stretches. Heat pumps and mini-splits are growth terms, not the local volume leaders.

## SERP takeaway

Locals own brand queries. Category queries (“furnace repair Freeport IL”) are split between incumbents and mills. Almost nobody uses `/services/{job}-freeport-il/`. Climate is asserted, not sourced. Heat-pump dual-fuel for Zone 5A is underserved.

## Our stance

One Freeport-first IA. One configurable phone. Honest climate. No fabricated reviews, 24/7, licenses, or prices. Beat mills on honesty. Beat incumbents on navigation, problem-first UX, unique service pages, and a working conversion path.
