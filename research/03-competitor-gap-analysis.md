# Competitor gap analysis

## Gaps we can occupy

1. **Service + city slugs.** Locals use `/residential-ac-repair/` or `/cooling`. Mills use city subdomains. `/services/ac-repair-freeport-il/` is unused by strong locals.
2. **Climate with sources.** NOAA HDD/CDD and January normals, not “unique climate.”
3. **Problem-first homepage.** Symptom cards to guides/services, not a 6-card brochure.
4. **Split IA.** Repair vs install vs maintenance vs replacement as separate pages with unique copy.
5. **Heat pump honesty.** Zone 5A / dual-fuel conversation instead of Florida copy or anti-heat-pump rants.
6. **Mini-splits for older Freeport houses** (additions, rooms the trunks never reached).
7. **Working conversion.** Loescher’s schedule form was down. Bomar publishes multiple phones. We use one configurable CTA and a working form fallback.
8. **Chatbot that qualifies and calls**, with emergency safety, not a ChatGPT clone.
9. **No fake proof.** Do not invent Loescher’s 4.8/175, Bomar “BEST of,” or KIC template reviews.
10. **Speed and mobile drawer** vs template contractor sites.

## What we will not do

- Copy slogans (“Honest & Reliable,” “Clean it, fix it, replace it”).
- Publish competitor phones, licenses, or addresses as ours.
- Build 30 thin town pages.
- Claim 24/7, same-day, NATE, or years in business until configured.
- Compete with KIC by inventing reviews.
